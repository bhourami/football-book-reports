## Claude's pre-match reasoning

Tottenham missing Kudus, Maddison, Solanke, Simons (long-term ACL) and
Odobert — an unusually deep attacking-injury list for a side already
blunt in front of goal (0-0 v Everton in Matchweek 4). Villa also
missing Bailey, Abraham, Onana, Gomes and Manzambi, but Tottenham's
absences look more attack-critical, tipping this toward an away win.

On BTTS: both sides are missing significant attacking options, so goals
look harder to come by for either team than the market's ~58% fair
BTTS-yes price implies. Flagged as a larger-than-usual gap (9.7pp) from
a qualitative method — moderate confidence, not in the implausible
range that got Matchweek 4's Coventry BTTS declined, but worth watching.
