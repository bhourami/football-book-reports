## Claude's original pre-match reasoning (withdrawn)

~~Arsenal missing three first-choice central defenders at once —
Mosquera, Timber and Saliba — a genuine defensive crisis for the
league's best defence, not a minor absence.~~

**This was wrong, and a reader caught it, not the process.** Arsenal
are 6 wins from 6 in all competitions this season, including matches
missing this exact same back-three combination: Saliba has been out
long-term since before the season really got going (targeting a
November return), Timber was already out for most of those wins, and
Mosquera's more recent 3-game absence also fell during the winning run.
None of that is fresh, decision-relevant news — it's the status quo the
team has already won through repeatedly. The original reasoning read an
injury list without checking the team's actual results under the same
lineup situation.
