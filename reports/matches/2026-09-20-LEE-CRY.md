## The win pick, and why it was pulled

+22.6pp was the largest single edge recorded anywhere in this project —
bigger than Matchweek 4's Coventry-Brighton BTTS outlier, which turned
out to be a warning sign about the model, not a real discovery. This
one didn't survive the check: confirmed 2026-09-19 against the live
Premier League table, Leeds are 4th, unbeaten (2W-2D-0L). Crystal
Palace are 16th, one win in four. Backing the struggling team to win at
the biggest edge in the project's history, against an unbeaten top-4
side, is the same failure pattern as Brighton-Arsenal's "defensive
crisis" — a large claimed edge the actual results directly contradict.
Withdrawn, not re-priced.

Claude's own candidate on this fixture was Leeds to win, declined on
its own numbers — no conclusion bet was ever possible either way.

The owner asked for this pick to be checked especially hard given its
size, rather than passed through because the arithmetic cleared the
threshold. It didn't survive.

Rodon (hamstring) — Ilia Gruev, out for a long stretch with a knee

still 4.75), so the recomputed edge is identical: +22.6pp. The decline
holds on team news, form/table position, and price, together — nothing
in today's check gives grounds to revive it.
