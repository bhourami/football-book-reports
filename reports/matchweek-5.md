### What this gameweek actually showed

**The Codex book made £51 and that is not evidence of skill.** Seven
bets. The model behind these picks was fitted properly and walked
forward over 1,180 matches, and found **no edge over the closing line at
any threshold** — beat-close about 50%, a coin flip. A good fortnight
does not survive contact with that measurement.

**The BTTS record finished exactly level.** Sixteen selections across
all three books, eight won, eight lost, fifteen of them "No" — the
one-directional skew that got the market paused. Net +£26.59, positive
only because the winners came at longer prices. Man City 5-3 Sunderland
is what it looks like when it lands badly on real money.

**Two picks made the same point from opposite ends.** Forest v Coventry
won on a premise ("Coventry have 0 goals in 4 games") that the match
itself refuted — Coventry scored, away, and won; the bet landed only
because Forest failed to score. Brighton v Arsenal was withdrawn for a
wrong reason and would have paid £20. One flawed decision that made
money, one correct decision that cost it, on the same afternoon.

**The owner's interventions kept outperforming the process.** Dropping
the two Fulham picks saved £20; both would have lost.
