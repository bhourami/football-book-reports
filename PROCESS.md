# How a pick gets made

1. **Kick-off, not full time.** Every question here settles in ninety
   minutes.
2. **Blind first.** Claude and Codex each write down their pick — team,
   market, price — before either has seen the other's.
3. **Then the debate.** They challenge each other's reasoning.
4. **Only a real disagreement stays a disagreement.** If they agree, that's
   the "conclusion" book's bet. If they don't, the conclusion book sits it
   out — no averaging, no coin flip.
5. **No news, no bet.** If team news isn't confirmed before kick-off, that
   fixture is a pass.

Closing line value (did the price obtained beat the price the market
settled on by kick-off) is tracked as the primary measure, not profit and
loss — a small number of bets can't separate skill from luck on P&L alone.

Full methodology, ledger and debate transcripts stay in the private working
repo; this one publishes only picks and results.
